Cairn Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Cairn is an Android home-screen launcher developed by Jean Zen. This Privacy Policy applies to Cairn for Android wherever it is officially distributed.

This policy describes Cairn’s current data practices. Cairn’s distribution channel does not change how this policy applies. If Cairn’s data practices change, this policy will be updated accordingly.

The short version

Cairn processes information locally on your device so it can work as a launcher and provide its intentional-pause, Future Notes, and River Report features.

Cairn currently does not transmit your Cairn settings, app selections, notes, interaction history, River Report information, or custom wallpaper off your device.

Cairn currently has:

  • no user accounts;
  • no cloud sync;
  • no analytics;
  • no automatic crash reporting;
  • no advertising or tracking; and
  • no automatic remote-update checking.

Contacting Cairn by email is a separate, user-initiated communication path explained below.

Information Cairn processes on your device

Installed and launchable apps

To work as a launcher, Cairn reads information about apps that Android makes available as launchable apps. This can include:

  • app name and icon;
  • package and component identifiers; and
  • the Android user or profile associated with the app.

Cairn uses this information to show apps in its launcher, let you configure apps for Home or the intentional pause, and open an app when you select it.

The complete launchable-app list is read dynamically rather than saved as a persistent inventory. Cairn does save information about apps you configure, such as apps placed on Home or selected for the intentional pause.

Cairn also observes when apps are installed or removed so its local launcher list can remain current.

If you choose an uninstall action from Cairn, Cairn asks Android to display the system uninstall flow. Android controls the confirmation and removal.

Home apps and widgets

Cairn stores the Home configuration you create, including information such as:

  • selected apps and widgets;
  • the Android profile associated with a selected app;
  • layout order, position, and size; and
  • tile appearance and custom labels.

This information remains on your device.

Settings, appearance, and custom wallpaper

Cairn stores local settings needed to preserve your chosen experience, including:

  • theme and onboarding state;
  • Home and guidance progress;
  • wallpaper choice;
  • River Report and Future Notes guidance state; and
  • other launcher preferences.

If you select a custom wallpaper image, Cairn reads that image locally and stores a local Android reference to it. Cairn does not upload the image.

StatusTags and Future Notes

Cairn stores the StatusTags and Future Notes you create or edit. These may contain text you write about your own state or intentions.

This content remains on your device and is not automatically sent to Jean Zen or any third party.

Intentional-pause and River Report history

When you enter Cairn’s intentional-pause flow, Cairn may store local history such as:

  • the time of the interaction;
  • the app involved;
  • how far the interaction progressed;
  • the state and Future Note shown during the interaction;
  • whether you continued or stepped back; and
  • relevant local completion or return timing.

Cairn also keeps local history of changes to states and Future Notes where needed to produce River Report views.

River Report is generated locally from this information. Cairn does not upload the report or maintain a cloud report history.

Cairn’s intentional pause operates only through Cairn’s own launcher paths. Cairn does not monitor activity inside other apps.

Information sent off your device

Cairn currently does not transmit its settings, installed-app information, configured apps and widgets, StatusTags, Future Notes, interaction history, River Report information, custom wallpaper, or other Cairn content off your device.

Cairn currently does not use this information for analytics, advertising, tracking, or automatic crash reporting. It does not sell this information or share it with third parties.

This describes data handling by the Cairn app. Downloading Cairn from a distribution service is a separate interaction between you and that service and is governed by the service’s own privacy practices.

Emailing Cairn

If you voluntarily email Cairn, Jean Zen will receive the information you choose to send, which may include:

  • your email address;
  • your message and subject;
  • attachments you choose to include; and
  • ordinary email delivery information.

This information is used to respond to support requests, feedback, or privacy inquiries.

Email is separate from Cairn’s local app behavior. Cairn does not automatically attach or send your device data, app list, settings, notes, or history when you contact support.

Your chosen email provider and the providers involved in delivering the message process the email according to their own terms and privacy practices.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed to handle the inquiry and maintain an appropriate support record. You may request deletion of Cairn’s copy of your correspondence by emailing the address below.

Retention and deletion

Cairn’s local information generally remains on your device until you change it, delete it, clear Cairn’s app storage, or uninstall Cairn.

Available controls include:

  • removing apps or widgets from Cairn Home;
  • removing apps from the intentional-pause selection;
  • editing or deleting current StatusTags and Future Notes; and
  • changing Cairn settings and appearance choices.

Deleting or changing a current StatusTag or Future Note does not necessarily remove text already preserved in earlier interaction history or historical snapshots used by River Report. Those local historical records remain until Cairn’s complete app data is deleted.

Reset Home does not delete all Cairn data. It removes Cairn-managed Home apps and widgets so you can configure Home again. It preserves unrelated information, including the wallpaper, intentional-pause selections, StatusTags, Future Notes, interaction and River Report history, theme, and other preferences.

To delete all Cairn-managed local data, clear Cairn’s storage through Android System Settings or uninstall Cairn. This removes Cairn’s local database and preferences. Cairn does not maintain an account or cloud copy that requires a separate deletion request.

Clearing Cairn’s data removes Cairn’s reference to a custom wallpaper but does not delete the original image from the location where you stored it.

Data security

Cairn stores its information within Android’s app-specific storage and relies on Android’s application isolation and your device’s access controls to protect that information.

Because Cairn currently keeps its app data on your device, it does not transmit that data to a Cairn server.

The security of local information also depends on your device, Android configuration, and device access controls. Information you voluntarily send by email is instead handled through the email services involved in delivering and storing the message.

Changes to this policy

If Cairn’s data practices change, this policy will be revised and the Last updated date will change.

The current policy will remain available at the same canonical public Privacy Policy URL. Material changes may also be identified in applicable Cairn release information.
